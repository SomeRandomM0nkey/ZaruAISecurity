About ZaruAI

Bridging the gap between AI innovation and educational safety

Our Mission

ZaruAI is dedicated to making artificial intelligence accessible and safe for educational environments. We understand that schools need powerful AI tools while maintaining strict security, privacy, and age-appropriate content controls.

Why ZaruAI?

Traditional AI tools weren't designed for educational settings. ZaruAI provides a comprehensive platform that gives educators the benefits of AI technology while ensuring student safety, data privacy, and appropriate content filtering.
